# Loftie

**Better sleep, without your phone on the nightstand.** Loftie makes bedside devices and a companion app that help people wind down, sleep deeper and wake up refreshed. The Loftie Clock is New York Times Wirecutter's best alarm clock, six years running.

- Shop: [loftie.com](https://loftie.com)
- Founded in 2018 by Matt Hassett

## Products

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Loftie Clock** | A phone-free alarm clock and wellness device. Wind-down sounds and content, a two-phase alarm that starts soft, and everything you used your phone for at bedtime, without the phone. Also in a Red Shell edition. |
| **Loftie Lamp** | A minimalist bedside lamp and wake-up light: a personal sunrise in the morning, warm low light at night, and modes for day, reading and night, plus custom modes down to a melatonin-friendly red glow. Pairs with the Clock. |
| **Loftie Card** | A wallet-sized card that locks your phone. Tap it and the apps and sites you chose go quiet until you tap again. One-time purchase, no subscription; works on iPhone and Android with the Loftie app, no clock needed. |
| **Loftie app** | Controls the Clock and Lamp, runs Loftie Card sessions, and carries Loftie's library of sounds and wind-downs. iOS and Android. |
| **Night Lights** | Light-sensing amber night lights that turn on at dusk and off at dawn, matching the Lamp's night mode. |
| **Sleep goods** | Sleep mask, mouth tape, Dark Dots light-blocking stickers, *The Mysteries of Sleep* book, bedtime spray and more. |

## How we build

Loftie is a small team that builds hardware, firmware, apps and commerce together: ESP32 firmware on the devices, a Flutter app, cloud services behind them, and a Shopify storefront. Loftie runs its day-to-day operations on [Deliberate](https://github.com/Deliberate-Studio), an AI agent team for consumer brands.

Public code: [esp_provisioning](https://github.com/Deliberate-Digital/esp_provisioning), the Flutter library we use for ESP32 Wi-Fi provisioning.
